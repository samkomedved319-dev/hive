Meet the Buddy. It lives at your cursor.

Enable Hive Buddy at Cursor in Settings. An orange companion trails your mouse — breathing, blinking, watching. It pops an island when it listens, thinks, clicks, or types, and hides the moment you hide Hive. Tray → Ask Hive opens the notch without the full window.

Buddy

Trails the mouse on every screen and hides whenever Hive hides. Pick its ink: Orange, Ink, Ghost, Cobalt, Amber.

Notch

Ctrl+Shift+J pops a pill at the top of the screen. Ask, dictate, or run slash commands. Esc dismisses.

PC control

The buddy opens apps, runs shell, clicks, and types — after you Allow. Destructive commands are blocked.

Swarm

Scout, Hive, Pulse, Critic, and Sentry on one message. A swarm strip shows when two or more overlap.
